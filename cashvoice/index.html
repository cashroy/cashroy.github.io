Voice changer + soundboard · Windows

CashVoice.

Change your voice and play sounds straight into your real microphone, system-wide. Discord, OBS, Teams and games just use your mic as normal, with no virtual cable.

Download for Windows View on GitHub

Free · Windows 10 & 11 · x64 and ARM64 · Early release (v0.1)

Features

Everything in one tray app.

01

Voice changer

Pitch, robot, echo, filters and distortion. Ten built-in voices, plus your own.

02

Your real mic

An audio effect hooks the microphone itself, like Equalizer APO. Every app picks it up automatically.

03

Soundboard

Import files or drag and drop. Sounds play into the mic, and optionally on your speakers too.

04

YouTube import

Paste a link and the audio is downloaded straight into your soundboard.

05

Pitch slider

Fine-tune any voice from −24 to +24 semitones.

06

Hotkeys for everything

Voice on/off, next voice, mute, stop sounds, and one key per voice and per sound.

Setup

Ready in a minute.

  1. Install

    Run CashVoice-Setup.exe and allow the admin prompt.

  2. Settings

    Open CashVoice from the tray and go to Settings.

  3. Tick your mic

    Tick the microphone(s) you talk into.

  4. Apply

    Click Apply. Windows audio restarts for a second. Then restart any app that was already using the mic.

Heads up: the installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you. Choose More info → Run anyway. To undo everything, untick your mic and Apply, or just uninstall.
