CashVoice.

Change your voice and play sounds through your actual mic. Discord, OBS and games pick it up like normal. No virtual cable to set up.

Early release · Free · Windows 10 & 11 · x64 and ARM64

On your phone? Open this page on your Windows PC to install it.

What it does.

Voice changer

Pitch, formant, robot, echo, filters, distortion. Nine voices built in, or make your own.

Uses your real mic

It hooks into your mic directly, so every app hears the effect without changing any settings.

Soundboard

Drag sounds in and play them into your mic. You can hear them on your speakers too if you want.

YouTube import

Paste a YouTube link and it lands in your soundboard.

Hear yourself

Listen to your changed voice in your headphones while you tweak it, with or without the effect.

Hotkeys

Bind a key to anything: each voice, each sound, mute, stop.

Setup.

  1. Install

    Run CashVoice-Setup.exe and allow the admin prompt.

  2. Settings

    Open CashVoice from the tray and go to Settings.

  3. Tick your mic

    Tick the microphone(s) you talk into.

  4. Apply

    Click Apply. Windows audio restarts for a second. Then restart any app that was already using the mic.

Heads up: the installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you. Choose More info → Run anyway. To undo everything, untick your mic and Apply, or just uninstall.
