AI notes organiser · Windows

UniNote.

All your uni notes, sorted by year, semester and paper. Drop a file in, Claude figures out what it is and files it, then helps you study it.

Download installer Portable .exe

Latest release · Free · Windows 10 & 11 · The installer updates itself, and the portable version runs without installing.

What it does.

Uses your Claude subscription. No API key needed.

Year → Semester → Paper

Your degree laid out in the sidebar. Add years, semesters and papers as you go.

Drop anything

Lecture notes, assignments, past papers. Claude works out what each one is and you confirm before it's filed.

Summaries

Study sheets, outlines or exam revision notes, saved next to the original file.

Tests

Pick what a test covers, add a past paper, and get practice questions and condensed notes.

Flashcards

Flip through cards and grade yourself. Spaced repetition brings them back when they're due.

Calendar

Tests and due dates on a month view, plus your weekly timetable. Upload your timetable and it gets filled in.

Search everything Ctrl K

Search every file, note and summary across your whole degree.

Ask Claude

Ask questions about a paper or semester and get answers from your own notes.

Your files stay yours

It's all normal folders on your PC. Open them in Explorer whenever.
