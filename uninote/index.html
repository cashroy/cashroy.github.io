AI notes organiser · Windows

UniNote.

Your whole degree in one place. Organise notes by Year → Semester → Paper, drop in any file, and Claude works out what it is, files it, and helps you study it.

Download installer Portable .exe

Latest release · Free · Windows 10 & 11 · The installer updates itself, and the portable version runs without installing.

Features

Study smarter, not messier.

Runs on your Claude subscription. Sign in with Claude, with no API key and no pay-per-use.

01

Year → Semester → Paper

A clean degree tree in the sidebar. Add years, semesters and papers inline.

02

Drop anything

Drag in lecture notes, workshops, assignments or past papers. Claude classifies each one, and you confirm before it's filed.

03

Summaries

Study sheets, outlines, plain-language explainers or exam revision, saved right next to the original.

04

Tests

Tick what a test covers, attach a past paper, and get a topic map, condensed notes and practice questions.

05

Flashcards

Playable flashcard sets with spaced repetition that brings cards back right when they're due.

06

Calendar

Every test and due date on a month grid, plus a weekly timetable that Claude builds from your uploaded timetable.

07

Search everything Ctrl K

Find any document, note, summary or test across your whole degree in seconds.

08

Ask Claude

Ask questions about a paper, semester or year. Answers come from your own documents.

09

Your files stay yours

Everything is a plain folder tree on your PC. Open it in Explorer any time.
